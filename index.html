HOW A REPORT IS BUILT 5 STAGES
1
Collect
Public and licensed sources are searched with a defined purpose and scope. No collection without consent, no untraceable dead ends.
2
Resolve & extract
Findings are tied to a single identity and turned into individually-cited claims — nothing enters the report without a source behind it.
3
Verify — multiple agents
Every claim is checked against its evidence, then cross-reviewed by several independent AI models on different providers — never approved on one model's word.
4
Human expert review
A person — not just the two AI models — reads flagged items and approves the case before anything reaches you.
5
Deliver
A narrative report with a full citation trail — plus a GEDCOM family tree file for ancestry work.
